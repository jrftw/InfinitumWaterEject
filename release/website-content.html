Frequently asked questions

Can water-ejection audio fix a damaged speaker?

No repair is guaranteed. A tone may help move droplets close to a speaker, but cannot remove internal moisture or reverse damage. Persistent problems need the manufacturer's advice or professional service.

Does this app clear the charging port?

No. It does not clean or dry charging ports. Do not charge a wet connector. Follow Apple's liquid-detection guidance.

Does it work on a nearby Watch or AirPods?

This release plays sound through the iPhone or iPad running the app. Disconnect headphones, Bluetooth and AirPlay before using tones. It does not remotely clean another device.

Does the app measure whether water is gone?

No. The progress indicator measures time, and the completion percentage measures finished sessions. Neither measures dryness or repair success.

What devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 16 or later.

Use a low starting volume and keep the speaker away from your ears. Do not use heat, compressed air, rice or objects in ports. Stop if uncomfortable.
